Operational Problem

Small businesses get overcharged for slow Wix/WordPress sites with broken forms, unverified email DNS, and dead blogs—and rural properties need physical enclosures, solar battery packs, and radio nodes that survive Ontario winters.

Engineered System

I also built harwick-route, an interactive hand-painted map portfolio of Harwick's 2026 paving season. For hardware jobs, I model and 3D-print weather-ready enclosures, solar-backed Meshtastic roof nodes, and ESP32 interfaces.
