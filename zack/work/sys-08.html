Three formats, one pipeline

A product-led commercial, a kinetic-typography film and an archival history short. Each uses the same production approach: verified source material, deliberate editing and precise, code-rendered graphics.

The films

Harwick Paving: a 24-second commercial built from real job photographs animated with Google Flow / Veo and finished in Framefields.

NOW IS THE CONTEST: Epictetus in motion — typography, narration and an orchestral score.

Before the Paver: an 82-second history short presented by Harwick. Five City of Toronto Archives photographs show Beverley Street on 12 October 1910. AI-generated movement and scene sound are clearly identified as reconstruction, alongside the original photographs and archive credits.
